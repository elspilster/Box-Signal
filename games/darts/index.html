How to win

Start on 501 and count down to exactly zero. Your winning dart must hit a double or the inner bull (50). Going below zero, leaving 1, or reaching 0 without a double is a bust — your score resets to the start of that visit.

On desktop, press Space to throw. On mobile, tap the board directly or use Throw Dart.
