JUST THERE.

He’s not a toolbar, not a dashboard, and not a productivity cult in a black turtleneck. He’s just there: floating beside your work, ready to chat, help, or simply keep you company while you think.

Built to feel calm, personal, and a bit unusual, Dot Bot is designed for people who spend long hours at a computer and wouldn’t mind a little presence in the room. Sometimes that means answering a question. Sometimes it means talking through an idea. Sometimes it means saying nothing at all.

Dot Bot isn’t here to replace people, or pretend to be more than he is. He’s a companion for the in-between moments — the browser tabs, the documents, the late-night code, the odd silence after a busy day.

A small black sphere with two eyes and a personality, hanging around because sometimes that’s enough.

Friendly

Talk naturally. Dot Bot is built around conversation and companionship rather than menus full of features.

Present

Dot Bot floats beside what you’re doing without trying to take over your workspace.

Simple

We deliberately haven’t packed him with everything imaginable. The companion is the heart of Dot Bot.

YOU'RE IN CONTROL.

Dot Bot is AI software, not a person, and AI responses can be wrong. He should be clear about what he can and cannot do, never pretend an action happened when it didn't, and keep access to your computer within clear limits. Don't enter passwords, banking details or authentication codes into Dot Bot, and verify important medical, legal, financial or safety information independently.
