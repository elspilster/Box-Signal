BEHIND THE SOUND

The artist

Like3 Mad is a music project with a taste for movement, colour and the unexpected. My sound leans into the possibilities of electronic music: pulsing beats, layered textures, restless rhythms and melodies that invite the listener to get lost in the moment.

Rather than asking every track to fit neatly into a box, Like3 Mad treats music as a space for experimentation. The result is an approach driven by curiosity and feeling, where a groove can take the lead, a sound can become a story, and the energy matters as much as the destination.

Wavelength is a home for that creative spirit — a place to listen, discover and follow the music as the collection grows. Whether you're here for a quick burst of energy or a longer listening session, you're welcome on the frequency.

Different sounds. One wavelength.
