Introducing Purple Hippo — Powered by Bubbles 🫧

Here at Box Signal, we've been busy developing some exciting new projects, and we're delighted to begin sharing them with you.

We're particularly pleased to introduce Purple Hippo, our new search engine, powered by our very own Bubbles technology.

Bubbles is an independent web-crawling and indexing system that we're developing to discover, collect and organise information from across the internet.

Together, Purple Hippo and Bubbles represent an exciting new direction for Box Signal: moving beyond creative content and into the development of our own internet technologies.

It's still early days, but we're already seeing encouraging progress as Bubbles explores a growing variety of websites and builds its searchable index.

And this is only the beginning.

We'll have more developments and new projects to share with you soon.
